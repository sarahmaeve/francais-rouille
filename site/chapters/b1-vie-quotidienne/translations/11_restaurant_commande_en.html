Ordering at the Restaurant

B1 — English Translation

Characters

  • Émilie — a customer having dinner with a friend
  • Julien — the restaurant's waiter
Julien: Good evening, ladies. Welcome to Léon. Do you have a reservation?
Émilie: Yes, I booked a table for two under the name Émilie Moreau.
Julien: Perfect, it's the table by the window. Follow me, please.
Émilie: Thank you. Do you have a daily set menu?
Julien: Yes, today we're offering onion soup as a starter, duck confit as the main course, and apple tart for dessert.
Émilie: That sounds delicious. I'll have the daily set menu, please.
Julien: Very good. And for your friend?
Émilie: She's going to look at the menu. Do you have any vegetarian dishes?
Julien: Of course. We have a mushroom risotto and a warm goat-cheese salad.
Émilie: The risotto — she'll like that. And for drinks, what do you recommend with the duck?
Julien: I'd suggest a glass of Cahors — it's a red wine from the South-West that goes very well with the confit.
Émilie: Perfect, two glasses of Cahors then. Thank you very much!
Julien: Noted. I'll bring your drinks right away.
